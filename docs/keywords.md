## Roman Urdu and spelling variants

- **Roman Urdu:** one real instance — the Facebook title "Keran to Taobat jeep rent **Kitna
  he**?" ("how much is…"). `taobat mein hotel` produced only generic chips (hotels / rates /
  price / contact number), and `neelum valley mein` drifted to "neelam valley pakistan". No
  evidence that Roman Urdu queries are common enough to write for.
- **"Neelam"** is a common spelling: "neelam valley hotels", Booking.com "Hotels near Neelam",
  "neelam valley pakistan". Worth using once, naturally, in body copy — not in titles.
- **"Taobut" / "Taobutt"** appear in competitor titles. Not worth using.
